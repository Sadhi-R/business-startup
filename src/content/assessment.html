Free, as described

Business readiness, without a trick test.

One announcement calls it a business readiness assessment and says it is not an exam: there are no right or wrong answers. Another calls the first step a free assessment.

This page does not contain a scored questionnaire. The project files do not include the assessment questions, so inventing them would be misleading. You tell us about your idea, and a person replies.

What to send

  • Who you are and how to reach you
  • The business idea, if you have one
  • What feels stuck
  • What you hope the next two months would change

Use the enquiry form and choose “Business readiness assessment”, or WhatsApp {{whatsapp}}.

Send an assessment enquiry

What it is not

It is not a credit check, an investor meeting, or a certificate. It does not tell you that a business will succeed. It is a way for the team to understand whether the school is a fit, and for you to ask what the two months actually involve.
