Programme

Two months, one plan.

The registration script describes Business Startup School as a two-month journey with {{mentor}}, {{role}}. Sending this website’s form does not reserve a place.

No price for this programme appears in the project files. The readiness step is described as free. If the two-month school has a fee, it will be written down before you are asked to pay.

What you work on

Idea

Name it

Look at the business idea in plain language. The posters ask whether you have one, and whether you can see it as a business.

Plan

Write it

Move from the idea to a business plan. One announcement puts it as Idea, then Plan, then Business.

Grow

Learn the work

Marketing and growth are named as part of the teaching. This is education, not a guarantee of sales.

Connect

Meet people

Mentors, professionals, and other entrepreneurs, as the posters describe the community around the school.

What the registration form asks

The existing form script collects:

  • Full name
  • Father or mother’s name
  • A 10-digit mobile number
  • Email address
  • Address
  • The problem you want these two months to change
  • The dream you are aiming at
  • Agreement to join for two months and follow the guidelines the team shares

Use call or WhatsApp to ask for a conversation. The fuller registration is completed when the team confirms the next step.
