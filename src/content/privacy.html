Privacy policy

This notice explains what this website does with information you send through the enquiry form. It is not a certificate of legal compliance. A qualified adviser should review it before you treat it as formal legal advice.

Last updated 28 October 2026.

Who receives enquiries

Business Startup School enquiries are handled through the contact form and the call, WhatsApp, and email icons on this website. A registered organisation name, GST number, and postal address were not in the project materials, so they are not stated here. The mentor named in the programme materials is {{mentor}}.

What the form collects

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • The enquiry type and the message you write
  • The time of submission
  • Your IP address and browser description, used to limit spam

Calls, WhatsApp messages, and emails contain whatever you choose to send.

Why it is used

We use these details to reply, to keep a record of the conversation, and to reduce repeated or abusive submissions. We do not sell enquiry data. We do not add you to a marketing list unless you ask to hear about the school.

Email

The form is processed on the server. A notification is sent to the operator’s configured mailbox. Your address is the Reply-To, so a reply can reach you. Mail passwords stay in server environment variables and are not written into the website.

How long it is kept

Messages are kept long enough to respond and for ordinary records of enquiries. You can ask for deletion using the email icon on the Contact page. A copy may be kept if it is needed for a dispute or a legal duty.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not use a third-party analytics product. The spam limit lives in the server’s memory while the application is running. It is not a cookie on your device.

Children

Students may be interested in the school. Do not send a child’s details unless you are the parent or guardian and the message is about them.

Requests

You may ask what we hold, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it, using the email or call icon on the Contact page. This page does not claim a particular statutory deadline.
