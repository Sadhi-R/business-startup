About

A school for the first serious plan.

Business Startup School introduces itself, in its own registration form, as a two-month journey. The mentor named throughout the materials is {{mentor}}, {{role}}.

What the materials say

Posters invite people to recognise a business idea, turn it into a plan, learn about marketing and growth, and connect with mentors, professionals, and other entrepreneurs. The registration form asks for a name, a parent’s name, a phone number, an email address, an address, the problem you want to work on, and the dream you are aiming at.

It also asks for agreement to join for two months and to follow the programme guidelines the team shares. Those guidelines are not printed in the project files, so they are not reproduced here. They should be given to you in writing before you commit.

Dhruva Foundation

Several announcements place a Dhruva Foundation mark next to Business Startup School and use the line “Learn | Build | Create Impact.” This site records that the materials do so. It does not state that the foundation owns, funds, or accredits the school, because that is not written down in the files we have.

What we will not claim

There are no published testimonials, pass rates, income figures, or certificates in the project files. None are shown on this website. Photographs of groups that appear inside promotional posters are treated as artwork, not as proof of a cohort.
